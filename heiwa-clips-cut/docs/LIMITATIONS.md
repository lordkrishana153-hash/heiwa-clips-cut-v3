# Known Limitations

Browser, WebCodecs, memory, and device limitations will be documented continuously across Stages 1–10.

- **WebCodecs requirement**: Local encoding and decoding require a browser supporting WebCodecs in a secure context (`https://` or `http://localhost`).
- **Codec availability**: H.264/AAC and VP9/Opus encoder availability varies by OS, GPU drivers, and browser build.
- **CDN dependency on first visit**: Mediabunny `1.62.0` is loaded on demand from `https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/mediabunny@1.62.0/+esm` and cached by the Service Worker (`sw.js`) for offline reuse. If opened offline before the first online visit or behind a blocked CDN, video analysis displays a clear error instead of crashing; see `docs/README.md` to vendor it locally.
- **Variable frame rate (VFR) estimation**: Frame rate is estimated from up to 50 packets via `computePacketStats(50)` with a 30 fps fallback if stats cannot be read; VFR phone recordings will display an average frame rate.
- **15-second load timeout**: Very slow network-mounted or cloud-placeholder video files that do not emit `loadedmetadata` within 15 seconds fail cleanly with `E_LOAD_TIMEOUT`.
- **Canvas `ctx.filter` blur support**: The Fit-mode blurred background option requires browser support for Canvas 2D `ctx.filter = 'blur(...)'`. The app detects real pixel blur support at runtime and hides the **Blur** option (falling back to black bars) in browsers that do not support it.
- **Even output dimensions**: Video encoders (H.264/VP9) require even pixel dimensions. Computed crop output widths and heights are rounded to the nearest even integer (`>= 2` and `<= maxSide`), which may shift target aspect ratios by at most 1 pixel.
- **Fast mode keyframe alignment**: Fast mode copies encoded packets directly without re-encoding, starting from the nearest earlier keyframe (`getKeyPacket(startSec)`). Clips may begin slightly before the chosen start point, and Fast mode is disabled whenever crop, zoom, or aspect ratio reframing is active or when source codecs do not fit the chosen container.
- **Worker `OffscreenCanvas` requirement for crop**: Cropped/reframed Precise exports draw frames onto an `OffscreenCanvas` inside the module Web Worker. Browsers without `OffscreenCanvas` in workers cannot export cropped clips.
- **In-memory single-clip output buffer**: Mediabunny writes each exported clip into a `BufferTarget` before handing back the `Blob`. Very long, high-bitrate single clips can hit browser RAM limits (especially on phones); preflight warns or blocks with `E_OUT_OF_MEMORY_RISK` when estimated clip size exceeds safe thresholds.
- **Standard 32-bit ZIP limit (4 GB)**: Store-only ZIP creation checks the total archive size before writing and refuses batches over ~4 GB (`ZIP_MAX_BYTES = 4,000,000,000`) with `E_ZIP_TOO_LARGE`. Download clips individually for very large batches.
- **Browser multi-download blocking**: When clicking **Download All**, some browsers prompt for "Automatic downloads" permission after the first file or throttle rapid downloads. If files are blocked, allow automatic downloads for this site or click each clip's individual **Download** button.
- **Mobile ZIP memory**: On browsers without `showSaveFilePicker` (most phones, tablets, Firefox, Safari), ZIP archives assemble headers and clip Blobs into a single download Blob. On phones, individual downloads are recommended over ZIP for large clip batches.
- **Output verification duration tolerance**: Precise-mode exports are verified to have non-zero size, readable `<video>` metadata with non-zero dimensions, and duration within `0.5s` of the requested range. Fast-mode exports may start earlier at the nearest keyframe, so verification checks that duration is positive and not truncated below `expected - 0.5s`.
- **Re-selecting video files on reload**: Browsers do not allow standard web apps to silently reopen arbitrary local video files after a page reload. Recovery restores clip ranges, framing, and export settings from IndexedDB, then asks you to re-select the local video and verifies name, size, and duration match before re-attaching clips.
- **Private browsing & storage eviction**: In private/incognito modes or strict privacy profiles where IndexedDB is blocked or quota is full, autosave turns off cleanly (`E_STORAGE_UNAVAILABLE` / `E_STORAGE_FULL`) while editing and exporting continue in memory. Even when `navigator.storage.persist()` is requested, browsers may keep storage as "best-effort" until the site is installed as a PWA or bookmarked.
- **Offline first-visit requirement**: Service Worker precaching and Mediabunny (`1.62.0`) CDN caching require at least one online visit over HTTPS (or `http://localhost`). If the app was opened online once, subsequent launches and local video clipping work offline; if opened completely offline before Mediabunny was cached, video analysis will report an error until online once (or until Mediabunny is vendored locally).
- **iPadOS/iOS install flow**: Safari on iPad/iPhone does not fire `beforeinstallprompt`; the header button shows an **Add to Home Screen** guide using Safari's Share menu instead.
- **TWA full-screen verification**: Packaging as an Android TWA removes the Chrome URL bar only after `.well-known/assetlinks.json` is updated with your real package name and SHA-256 certificate fingerprint(s) (including Google Play App Signing) on your HTTPS domain.
